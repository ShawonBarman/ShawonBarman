## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Founding Engineer at [TMSEZ Inc.](https://tmsez.com)**, a US-based trucking & logistics SaaS, where I lead **full-stack development** across the platform — from real-time dispatch systems and authentication to third-party integrations and AI-powered automation.

- 🏗️ **Full-stack:** React / TypeScript, Node.js / Express, Django, Flask, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Socket.IO, Redis
- 🔌 **Integrations:** ELD telematics, EDI (X12), mailbox systems (Gmail & Outlook), payment gateways, and REST APIs
- 🤖 **AI-powered features:** document intelligence, OCR automation, and generative-AI integrations in production workflows
- 🔬 **Researcher:** IEEE-published in deep learning — CNN-based medical & agricultural image classification (COVID-19, autism & plant-disease detection)
- 🧩 **Problem solver:** 543+ challenges solved on competitive programming platforms
- 🌍 Open to remote & international opportunities

---

## 🚀 What I'm Working On

- Architecting and scaling a production SaaS used by US trucking companies
- Building real-time dispatch features, SSO/session authentication, and ELD & EDI integrations
- Integrating **generative AI** and **document-intelligence** into real business workflows
- Sharing reusable, open-source projects that help other developers
